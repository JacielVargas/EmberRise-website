The Journey

Built one iteration at a time.

There was no single breakthrough. Just questions, experiments, failures, adjustments— and the decision to keep building.

2020

The Curiosity

Trading enters the picture.

What began with a few videos became genuine curiosity. Charts led to questions. Questions led to learning how markets move, how momentum develops, and how traders search for opportunity within it.

2020–2022

Learning the Market

Learning before building.

Paper trading. Technical analysis. EMA. SMA. RSI. MACD. TTM Squeeze. Patterns. Momentum. Different traders offered different ideas, and the pieces that made sense slowly became part of my own understanding. There was no system yet. I was simply learning.

The Pause

Life Gets Busy

The idea moved into the background.

Responsibilities grew and time became harder to find. Trading gradually moved into the background, and for a while the belief that I could actually make it work began to fade. But the idea never completely disappeared.

The Return

A Different Question

What if I could build my own system?

New technology changed the way I approached trading. Instead of only asking how to find the right trade, I began asking whether the tools and indicators I had learned over the years could be combined into something built specifically around the way I wanted to trade.

2025–2026

Build. Test. Fail. Refine.

The system begins taking shape.

First came custom indicators. Then scanners. Then filters, scoring systems, and custom order strategies. Ideas were tested against the market. Some worked. Many failed. Each failure exposed another question, another adjustment, and another version to test.

2026

EmberRise

The idea gets a name.

What had started as a personal trading project was becoming something more meaningful. EmberRise became the name for the idea: something small, given enough persistence and the right conditions, can grow into something far greater than where it began.

2026

Making It Real

The idea moves beyond the screen.

EmberRise LLC was formed. The domains were secured. The logo and visual identity were created. The website was built from the ground up. What had existed mostly as an idea now had a name, an identity, and a place in the real world.

Today

Still Building

The next milestone has not been written yet.

The system is still being developed. The scanners are still being refined. Strategies are still being tested. Every market session creates new information, and every failure reveals something that can be improved. The goal is not to pretend the answer has already been found. It is to keep searching for it.
